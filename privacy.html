Privacy Policy – Karakhana Manager

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Summary

  • Karakhana Manager does not collect, sell or share any personal data.
  • There is no account, no login, no ads and no analytics.
  • Everything you enter stays on your own phone.

Data stored on your device

The app saves the following only in your phone's browser storage so that you can use it again later:

  • Your business name and your name
  • Karigar (worker) names, phone numbers, work logs, attendance, payments and material records
  • Vyapari (trader) names, phone numbers, lots, fabric stock and payments
  • Factory expenses
  • The company logo you choose, and your app PIN (stored as a hash)

This data never leaves your device unless you choose to share it (see below). Clearing the app's data or uninstalling it deletes it, so take a backup regularly.

Photos

If you add a company logo, the app opens your gallery only when you tap the button, and keeps the chosen picture on your phone. It is not uploaded anywhere.

Sharing, PDFs and backups

When you tap WhatsApp, PDF or Backup, the app prepares a message or file and hands it to your phone's share screen or WhatsApp. You decide where it goes. The app does not send it anywhere by itself.

The app downloads a PDF library (jsPDF) from a public CDN to create PDFs. No personal data is sent with that request.

Children

Karakhana Manager is a business tool for factory owners. It is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
